Welcome

Technology has always been about solving problems. For most of my career, that meant designing and building software using established engineering principles, modern development practices, and cloud technologies. Like many engineers, my journey began in traditional software development, creating applications, integrating systems, and helping organisations deliver reliable, scalable technology solutions.

Over time, however, a new wave of technology started to emerge.

Artificial Intelligence moved from being an interesting research topic to becoming a practical tool that engineers could use to solve real business challenges. What began as curiosity quickly became a significant area of focus for me. I found myself exploring large language models, AI-assisted development, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agentic systems, prompt engineering, and the broader challenges of adopting AI responsibly within the enterprise.

What fascinated me most wasn't the technology itself, but the shift in mindset it required.

Traditional software engineering is largely deterministic. Given the same inputs, we expect the same outputs every time. AI systems challenge that assumption. They introduce probability, uncertainty, and entirely new design patterns that require engineers to think differently about architecture, testing, governance, security, and user experience.

As a result, I found myself transitioning from being solely a software engineer to becoming an engineer focused increasingly on AI-enabled solutions. Today my work sits at the intersection of software engineering, architecture, cloud platforms, and AI engineering, helping organisations understand where AI creates genuine value and where conventional engineering approaches remain the better choice.

Why This Blog Exists

The AI landscape is evolving at an incredible pace. New models, tools, frameworks, and approaches emerge almost weekly. Amidst all the excitement, I often find that practical engineering perspectives can be difficult to find.

This blog exists to document that journey.

My goal is to share:

  • Lessons learned from working with AI technologies
  • Practical guidance for engineers and architects
  • Thoughts on AI strategy and adoption
  • Technical deep dives and experiments
  • Reflections on architecture, cloud, and software engineering
  • Observations on where AI succeeds—and where it falls short

Rather than focusing on hype, I aim to explore AI through the lens of an engineer: understanding how things work, evaluating trade-offs, and applying technology to solve real-world problems.

Join Me On The Journey

We're living through one of the most significant technological shifts of our generation. As engineers, architects, and technology leaders, we have a unique opportunity—and responsibility—to understand these changes and apply them thoughtfully.

This site is my attempt to capture that journey as it unfolds.

I hope you find something useful here. Whether you're a fellow engineer, architect, technology leader, or simply someone curious about AI, welcome. Let's learn together.
