About Me

I'm Gareth Williams, an Engineering Manager with a passion for building technology who solves real-world problems.

Over the course of my career, I've worked across a wide range of technologies, helping organisations design, build, and modernise software platforms. My experience spans software engineering, architecture, cloud technologies, and engineering leadership, with a particular focus on delivering scalable, maintainable, and resilient solutions.

I enjoy working at the intersection of technology and business, translating complex requirements into practical engineering outcomes. Whether I'm shaping architectural direction, mentoring teams, exploring emerging technologies, or writing code, my goal remains the same: creating solutions that provide genuine value.

More recently, I've been focusing on Artificial Intelligence and AI Engineering, exploring how organisations can safely and effectively adopt generative AI, agent-based systems, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and modern development practices. I'm particularly interested in helping teams understand both the opportunities and limitations of AI technologies, moving beyond hype to practical, measurable outcomes.

This site serves as both a personal knowledge base and a place to share thoughts, experiments, lessons learned, and observations from the worlds of software engineering, architecture, cloud computing, and AI.

When I'm not immersed in technology, I'm usually reading, learning about emerging trends, experimenting with new tools, or exploring better ways to build and deliver software.

If you'd like to connect, you can find me on LinkedIn, where I regularly share insights and engage with the wider technology community.
